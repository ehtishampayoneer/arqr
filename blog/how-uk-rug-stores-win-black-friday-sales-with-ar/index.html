How UK Rug Stores Win Black Friday Sales with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a rug store owner in London. It is the week of Black Friday, and the biggest shopping weekend of the year is about to start. Shoppers in London, Manchester, Edinburgh and Birmingham are hunting for deals, and rugs are on their lists, for the living room, for the dining room, for a new flat, and as gifts. A woman in Manchester wants a rug under her sofa before the guests arrive for Christmas. A couple in Edinburgh found the shop through a search and want a rug for the hallway. A man in Birmingham has been watching one rug for weeks and plans to buy it on Friday while the price is down. They like the rug, they like the price, and they still hesitate, because a rug bought from a screen can arrive the wrong size, and on Black Friday there is no time to send it back and try again. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page wins those Black Friday sales before they are lost.

Why Black Friday rug sales get lost

Three reasons drive them. First, the size. A rug that looked right in a product photo can sit too small under the sofa, or run too wide for the room, and the buyer only finds out when it is already on the floor. Second, the colour. The rug in your bright showroom can look different in her living room light, and a warm beige on screen can read cool grey on her floor. Third, the clock. Black Friday deals end on Monday, so a shopper who is not sure waits, and a shopper who waits misses the deal and buys nothing. At Black Friday all three hit at once, because shoppers are buying fast, for a hard date, with no time to measure twice.

What changes when they see it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three doubts in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her floor, and the rug appears at its true size, right where it would lie. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa and her coffee table. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own floor. Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the rug, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. A Black Friday shopper who can check the true size hesitates less, and hesitation is the whole problem in the busiest weekend of the year.

Where the view goes on Black Friday weekend

  • On every product page, next to the price. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-at-true-size link beside the buy button answers the question at the moment it matters.
  • In the Black Friday email and the sale banners. The link opens on a phone, which is where your deal hunters browse.
  • On the price tags in the shop. A QR code on the tag opens the same rug in AR. A visitor checks it at home and orders before the deal ends on Monday.
