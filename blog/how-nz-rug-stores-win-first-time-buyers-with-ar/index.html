How New Zealand Rug Stores Win First-Time Buyers with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a rug store owner in Christchurch. Her showroom holds beautiful rugs, but most of her website visitors have never bought a rug online. A woman in Wellington loves a rug on the product page. A man in Auckland wants one for his new flat. A couple in Dunedin found her through a search. They like the pattern, they like the price, and they still hesitate, because buying a rug they have never touched feels like a gamble. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page turns those first-time buyers into customers, and keeps the rug from coming back.

Why first-time buyers hesitate on rugs

Three doubts stop them. First, the size. A first-time buyer has no feel for rug sizes. A 3 by 2 metre rug sounds right until it lands in a small room and swallows the floor, or sits too small under the coffee table and looks lost. Second, the colour. The rug in your bright showroom can look different in her living room light, and she has no sample at home to check it against. Third, trust. She has never bought from you, never touched the rug, and the nearest rug shop to her sells something else. She needs a reason to believe the rug on the screen is the rug that will arrive.

What changes when she sees it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three doubts in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her floor, and the rug appears at its true size, right where it would lie. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa and her coffee table. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own floor. Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the rug, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. A first-time buyer who can check the true size hesitates less, and hesitation is the whole problem with a first order.

Where the view goes for first-time buyers

  • On every product page, next to the price. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-at-true-size link beside the buy button answers the question at the moment it matters.
  • In the welcome email or message you send after a first visit. The link opens on a phone, which is where your first-time buyers browse.
  • On the price tags in the shop. A QR code on the tag opens the same rug in AR. A first-time visitor checks it at home and orders when she gets back.
