How Australian Decor Stores Win Black Friday Sales with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a decor store owner in Melbourne. She sells wall art, mirrors, lamps and vases from one shop and a website. Black Friday is her biggest weekend of the year. The traffic triples. Her inbox fills with questions. And most of the visitors still leave without buying. The price is right. The piece is right. The doubt is the size.

That is the Black Friday problem for decor. A sale brings the browsers, but a flat photo cannot tell a shopper if the mirror fits her wall or if the lamp suits her side table. She loves the price, she likes the piece, and she still closes the tab, because a wrong size on a sale item feels worse than no sale at all. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page turns that traffic into orders, and keeps those orders from coming back in January.

Why Black Friday browsers hesitate on decor

Sale shoppers decide fast, but decor is not a small purchase. The questions in her head are simple, and a photo answers none of them.

First, will it fit. A mirror that looks right in your bright shop can swallow a small wall at home, or sit too small above the sideboard and look lost. Second, will the colour work in her light. A lamp that glows warm in your shop window can look cold in her living room, or clash with the sofa she already owns. Third, will it suit the room. A bold print looks great in a photo and can take over a small room in real life.

On Black Friday she has one more worry. The sale ends Sunday. If she waits to measure, the price is gone. If she buys blind, the return costs her the saving. So she does nothing, and your biggest weekend of the year ends with traffic and no orders.

What changes when she sees it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three questions in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her wall, and the piece appears at its true size, right where it would hang. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own furniture.

Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the piece, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. That is what turns a deal hunter into a buyer before the sale ends. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. Shoppers who can check the true size hesitate less, and sale shoppers hesitate most of all, because the clock is running.

Where the view goes for the sale weekend

  • In the sale emails and social posts. The link opens on a phone, which is exactly where your Black Friday shoppers browse.
