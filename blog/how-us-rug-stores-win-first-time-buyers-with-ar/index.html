How US Rug Stores Win First-Time Buyers with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a rug store owner in Denver. She sells hand knotted rugs, wool rugs, and washable rugs from one shop and a website. Her regular customers buy fast. They know their size, they know their colours, and they trust her. Her first-time buyers are a different story. They have never bought a rug online. They love a rug on her website, and then they stop. They have no idea if it will fit their room, and they have no one to ask.

That first-time buyer is most of the growth in US rug sales. She is furnishing her first home, or replacing the rug she has had for ten years. She is not comparing your rug with five others. She is deciding whether she can trust buying a rug from a screen at all. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page wins her trust, and her order.

Why first-time buyers hesitate

A rug is a big purchase for someone who has never bought one. The questions in her head are simple, and a photo cannot answer any of them.

First, will it fit. An 8 by 10 rug sounds right until it arrives and swallows the room, or sits too small under the sofa and looks lost. She has never measured for a rug before, and numbers on a listing do not help her picture it. Second, will the colour work in her light. A rug that glows in your bright shop can look dark in her living room, or clash with the sofa she already owns. Third, will the pattern suit the room. A bold pattern looks great in a photo and can take over a small room in real life, and a quiet pattern can vanish under a dark sofa.

She does not want to get it wrong. A rug is not a small thing to send back. It is heavy, it costs real money to ship, and the return feels like a failure. So she browses, she hesitates, and she buys nothing. Or she drives to a big box store, where she can see the rug on the floor, even if it is not the rug she wanted.

What changes when she sees it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three questions in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her floor, and the rug appears at its true size, right where it would lie. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own furniture.

Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the rug, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. That is what turns a nervous first-time buyer into a confident one. She stops asking if it will fit and starts asking when it will arrive. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. Shoppers who can check the true size hesitate less, and first-time buyers hesitate most of all, because they have never done this before.
