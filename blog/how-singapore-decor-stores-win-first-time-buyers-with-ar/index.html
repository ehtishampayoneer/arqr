How Singapore Decor Stores Win First-Time Buyers with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a decor store owner in Singapore. She sells wall art, mirrors, lamps and vases from one shop and a website. Most of her website visitors have never bought a decor piece online. A woman in Tampines loves a vase on the product page. A man in Jurong wants a floor lamp for his new flat. A couple in Tiong Bahru found her through a search. They like the piece, they like the price, and they still hesitate, because buying a decor piece they have never seen in their own room feels like a gamble. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page turns those first-time buyers into customers, and keeps the piece from coming back.

Why first-time buyers hesitate on decor

Three doubts stop them. First, the size. A first-time buyer has no feel for decor sizes. A vase that looks right on a product photo can crowd a small sideboard, and a floor lamp can sit too tall beside a low sofa. Singapore living rooms run small, so a few centimetres matter. Second, the colour. The piece in your bright showroom can look different in her living room light, and she has no sample at home to check it against. Third, trust. She has never bought from you, never seen the piece in her own room, and the nearest decor shop to her sells something else. She needs a reason to believe the piece on the screen is the piece that will arrive.

What changes when they see it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three doubts in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her sideboard or her wall, and the piece appears at its true size, right where it would stand. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa and her coffee table. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own floor. Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the piece, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. A first-time buyer who can check the true size hesitates less, and hesitation is the whole problem with a first order.

Where the view goes for first-time buyers

  • On every product page, next to the price. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-at-true-size link beside the buy button answers the question at the moment it matters.
  • In the welcome email or message you send after a first visit. The link opens on a phone, which is where your first-time buyers browse.
  • On the price tags in the shop. A QR code on the tag opens the same piece in AR. A first-time visitor checks it at home and orders when she gets back.

A first order placed with a true-to-size view behind it stays sold. The pieces that used to come back for size reasons stop coming back, because the buyer saw the true size before she ordered.
