How Australian Rug Stores Sell to Distant Shoppers with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a rug store owner in Sydney. Her best rugs sit in one showroom, but her buyers live all over Australia. A woman in Perth loves a rug on the website. A man in Brisbane wants one for his new house. A family in Hobart found her through a search. None of them can walk into the shop, and none of them will spend real money on a rug they cannot see in their own room.

That is the distant buyer problem for rug stores. A rug is the biggest piece in the room, and a flat photo cannot tell a shopper if it fits her floor, suits her sofa, or works with the light she has. She likes the pattern, she likes the price, and she still closes the tab, because a rug that arrives too big or too small is a costly mistake, and sending it back costs even more. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page turns those far away browsers into buyers, and keeps the sale from coming back.

Why distant shoppers hesitate on rugs

Distance adds three doubts to the normal ones. First, the size. She cannot stand on the rug, pace it out, or hold it against her sofa. A 3 by 2 metre rug sounds fine until it lands in a small room and swallows the floor, or sits too small under the coffee table and looks lost. Second, the colour. The rug in your bright showroom can look different in her living room light, and she cannot bring a sample home to check. Third, trust. She has never met you, never touched the rug, and the nearest rug shop to her sells something else. She needs a reason to believe the rug on the screen is the rug that will arrive.

What changes when she sees it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three doubts in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her floor, and the rug appears at its true size, right where it would lie. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa and her coffee table. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own floor. Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the rug, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. A distant shopper who can check the true size hesitates less, and distance is the biggest hesitation of all.

Where the view goes for distant buyers

  • On every product page, next to the price. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-at-true-size link beside the buy button answers the question at the moment it matters.
  • In the emails and messages you send after a shopper asks about delivery. The link opens on a phone, which is where your distant shoppers browse.
  • On the price tags in the shop. A QR code on the tag opens the same rug in AR. A visitor from out of town checks it at home and orders when she gets back.
