ARQR360 vs Shopify AR Apps: What a Home Decor Store Actually Gets from Each

Picture a home decor store owner in Melbourne. She sells table lamps, mirrors, vases, and wall art on Shopify and from one shop. Her customers keep asking the same thing: will this suit my room? She reads about AR for Shopify and finds two very different answers. The Shopify app store has AR viewer apps she can install in a click. And then there is ARQR360, a service that builds the whole AR catalogue for her. She wonders which one is actually meant for a store like hers.

This is the honest version.

What Shopify AR apps are

Shopify AR apps are small viewer apps on the Shopify app store. You install one and it adds a 3D viewer to your product pages, the kind that lets a shopper open the piece in AR on their phone. Public app listings put pricing anywhere from free to roughly $36 a month, depending on how many products you publish.

Here is the part the listing does not lead with. The apps are viewers only. They show AR views. They do not make them. You supply every 3D file yourself, in the format the app accepts, usually USDZ and GLB. If you have nothing but product photos, nothing appears in that viewer.

What ARQR360 is

ARQR360 is a done-for-you AR catalogue for independent retailers. You send photos of your bestselling products and their real listed dimensions. We produce true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) views from them, host them, and hand you links and QR codes that open the AR view in your customer's phone browser. No app. No 3D team. No files to manage on your side.

The two are different layers. The app is a display window. ARQR360 is the thing that fills the window.

Price: small subscription plus the modelling bill, vs one public flat rate

The app route looks cheap on the listing page, and the app itself is cheap. The real cost sits in the views. Every 3D file has to be commissioned from a 3D artist or a modelling service, and decor pieces are detailed work. Shop owners report roughly $100 to $500 per product depending on complexity. A store with 60 lamps, mirrors, and vases is looking at thousands before the first customer opens anything in AR. We worked the same maths in our pricing breakdown for decor stores.

ARQR360 publishes its price: $199 one-time setup and $19 a month, locked for life for the first 50 stores. All AR view production is included. There is no per-product creation fee and no per-view fee.

Setup effort: a project you run vs photos you send

The app route is a project you manage. You find 3D artists or a modelling service. You check every file against the real dimensions. You upload them to the app. When a new range arrives, you commission more files. Most of the work is not the app. It is the file pipeline, and it never ends.

With ARQR360 the project is: send photos, get a working AR catalogue back in about seven days. The links go on your product pages. The QR codes go in the shop. When a new lamp arrives, you send its photo.

True-to-size accuracy

AR only works if the piece appears at its real dimensions. A lamp even slightly off teaches the customer the tool cannot be trusted. On the app route, size accuracy is your job. You verify every commissioned file against the real piece, one by one. Miss one and shoppers learn to ignore the viewer.

With ARQR360, we produce the AR views from your product photos and the real listed dimensions, and we check scale before anything ships. The first view is free, so you can hold your phone next to the real piece and check it yourself.

The Shopify-only limit

Shopify AR apps live inside Shopify. They work on your Shopify product pages and nowhere else. If you also sell on Instagram, at weekend markets, or through a showroom, the AR stays on the website. ARQR360 hands you links and QR codes that work from anywhere: product pages, packaging, shelf tags, social posts, printed flyers. A code on the shelf tag lets a browsing customer check the piece at home and come back decided.

Who each one fits

Shopify AR apps make sense if:

  • You already have true-to-size 3D files of your products, or a designer who makes them
  • Your budget covers per-product modelling on an ongoing basis
  • You are happy to run the file pipeline yourself
  • Your store is Shopify only

ARQR360 is designed for stores like yours if:

  • You sell finished products and hold no 3D files
  • You want the AR live this month with no new hires
  • You need one public price with no surprises
  • You want the views to work outside Shopify too: QR codes, social, the shop floor

The question to ask before you sign anything

Add up the full annual cost, not the monthly fee. Who produces the AR views and from what source material, and what does a new range cost? What happens to the views if you stop paying? And does the customer need an app? The answers will tell you which route fits your store faster than any feature list.

One more number, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. Whichever route you take, giving shoppers a true-to-size view beats a flat photo.
