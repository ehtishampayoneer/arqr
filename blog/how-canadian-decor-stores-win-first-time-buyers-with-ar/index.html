How Canadian Decor Stores Win First-Time Buyers with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a decor store owner in Toronto. She sells lamps, mirrors, framed prints, and vases from one shop and a website. Her regular customers buy fast. They know her taste, they know their rooms, and they trust her. Her first-time buyers are a different story. They have never bought decor online. They love a piece on her website, and then they stop. They have no idea if it will suit their room, and they have no one to ask.

That first-time buyer is most of the growth in Canadian decor sales. She is setting up her first home, or replacing the lamp she has had for ten years. She is not comparing your lamp with five others. She is deciding whether she can trust buying decor from a screen at all. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page wins her trust, and her order.

Why first-time buyers hesitate

A lamp or a framed print is a real purchase for someone who has never bought one online. The questions in her head are simple, and a photo cannot answer any of them.

First, will it fit. A mirror that looks right in your bright shop can swallow a small wall at home, or sit too small above the sideboard and look lost. She has never measured for wall art before, and numbers on a listing do not help her picture it. Second, will the colour work in her light. A lamp that glows warm in your shop window can look cold in her living room, or clash with the sofa she already owns. Third, will the style suit the room. A bold print looks great in a photo and can take over a small room in real life, and a quiet piece can vanish against a dark wall.

She does not want to get it wrong. Decor is not a small thing to send back. It is fragile, it costs real money to ship across the country, and the return feels like a failure. So she browses, she hesitates, and she buys nothing. Or she drives to a big shop in town, where she can see the piece on the shelf, even if it is not the piece she wanted.

What changes when she sees it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three questions in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her wall, and the piece appears at its true size, right where it would hang. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own furniture.

Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the piece, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. That is what turns a nervous first-time buyer into a confident one. She stops asking if it will suit the room and starts asking when it will arrive. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. Shoppers who can check the true size hesitate less, and first-time buyers hesitate most of all, because they have never done this before.
