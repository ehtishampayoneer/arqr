How Canadian Rug Stores Turn Browsers Into Buyers with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a rug store owner in Vancouver. She has a good website, good photos, and steady traffic. People browse her rugs for weeks. They open the same pages again and again. Then most of them buy nothing. They are not put off by the price. They are stuck on one question, and it is the same question every time: will this rug fit my room?

A rug is a big buy, and a big piece of the room. A photo cannot answer the size question. A list of dimensions cannot answer it either. Most shoppers cannot picture a 9 by 12 rug from numbers on a screen. So they browse, they hesitate, and they leave. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page turns those browsers into buyers.

Why browsers stop at the size question

Think about how a rug shopper actually decides. She finds a rug she loves. She checks the price and likes it. Then she looks at her living room and tries to guess. Will the rug reach the sofa legs? Will it leave enough bare floor at the edges? Will the pattern feel too big in a small room? She cannot answer any of that from a photo, so she does the safe thing. She waits.

Waiting is where rug sales die. A shopper who waits usually buys from the store that answers the question first, or buys nothing at all. Your website has one job at that moment: answer the size question on the spot, while she is still looking at the rug. A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view does that. She taps the link, points her phone at her floor, and the rug appears at its true size. She walks around it. She sees it against her sofa and her coffee table. The guesswork is gone in seconds.

What the shopper sees changes everything

Three things turn a browser into a buyer. First, the rug at its true size, not a small photo. Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the rug, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. Second, the rug in her own light. Colours look different in her living room than they do in a studio photo, and she can see that before she pays. Third, the rug with her own things around it. A rug that looks great alone can look wrong next to her sofa. A rug that looks plain in a photo can look right once it sits under her table. She finds that out on her own floor, in a minute, with nobody watching.

That is the moment a browser becomes a buyer. She stops asking if it will fit and starts asking when it will arrive. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. Shoppers who can check the true size hesitate less, and rug shoppers hesitate most of all, because the rug is the biggest thing in the room.

  • Next to the price on every product page. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-at-true-size link beside the buy button answers the question at the moment it matters.
  • On the price tags in the shop. A QR code on the tag opens the same rug in AR. A customer who likes a rug in the shop can check it at home that evening and order it from her sofa. No programming, no changes to your website needed. We send you a QR code for each product. Put it on the product page, on your packaging, in your shop, on social media, anywhere. Your buyer scans it and sees your product in their own room, real size.
  • In your posts and emails. The link opens on a phone, which is exactly where your shoppers browse. A short post with the link in it reaches browsers at the hour they actually shop.
