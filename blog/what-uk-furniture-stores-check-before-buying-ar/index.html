What UK Furniture Stores Should Check Before Buying AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a furniture store owner in Bristol. She sells sofas, armchairs and dining tables from one showroom and a website. Her shoppers keep asking the same question: will this piece fit my room. A sofa that is even a few centimetres off teaches the shopper the tool cannot be trusted. Ask every provider how they verify each view against the real listed dimensions of the piece, and ask to see the proof on your own bestselling piece before you pay for a catalogue.
