AR, Built Your Way: Custom AR Services for Stores That Want It Their Way

Picture a rug store owner in London. She sells 400 rugs online. Every AR company she talks to offers the same thing: pick a plan, upload your products, pay per month. The plan covers 50 products. She has 400. The next plan up covers 500 and costs triple, and includes things she will never use. She does not need a bigger box. She needs someone to build around her store.

That is the gap. Every AR offer on the market is a fixed plan. A fixed number of products, a fixed set of features, the same package whether you run a 40-piece decor shop or a 2,000-SKU furniture chain. The store bends to fit the plan. We think it should be the other way round.

AR, built your way

We are introducing something new: custom AR (Augmented Reality) services for retail stores. No fixed plans. No one-size package. You tell us exactly how you want AR to work in your store, and we build that. It comes in four shapes, and you pick the ones that fit, or ask for something else entirely.

1. Every product in AR, on autopilot

Every new item you add gets its true-size AR (Augmented Reality) view automatically. No uploads to remember, no extra work from your side. Your catalogue grows, and the AR grows with it. For stores that add new pieces every week, this is the difference between AR that covers the whole shop and AR that covers last season's bestsellers.

2. Custom AR for your store

AR pages and features designed around how your shoppers buy. A lighting store sells differently from a rug store. Your shoppers might need to see a chandelier from below at ceiling height, or a rug under their own dining table. Custom AR means the AR works the way your customers actually shop, not the way a template assumes they shop.

3. Even your own AR app too

Your store as a branded AR app on the App Store and Play Store. Some stores want their AR to live inside their own app, with their own name and their own branding. We build that. Your shoppers open your app, point their phone at the room, and see your products at true size.

4. We run your AR for you

Our team builds every model and handles the whole thing. You sell. This is the full managed route: we build the true-size AR views from your product photos, we host everything, we keep it running. You never touch a model, a dashboard, or a line of code.

For stores that want it their way

The point of all four is the same. Your store is not like the store next door, so your AR should not be either. Tell us what you are after and we will shape it around you. If none of the four fits exactly, say so. The whole idea is that we build around your needs, not the other way round.

One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. The mechanism is simple. Shoppers who can see the true size hesitate less.

See it on your own phone first. Our live furniture demo and live decor demo open in the browser with no install. For the maths behind the investment, read our pricing breakdown for furniture stores.

The cheapest way to check if it works for your store

You do not have to commit to anything to find out. Send a photo of your bestselling product and we will build you one true-to-size AR view free. Put it on your own phone, check it against the real piece, and see if your customers would use it. If it does not convince you, you have spent nothing.

FAQ

What does custom AR cost?

There is no fixed price, because there is no fixed package. You tell us what you need and we quote around that. The flat-rate catalogue plans still exist for stores that want them: $249 one-time setup and $29 a month, with all AR view production included. The first test view is free either way.

Do my customers need to download an app?

Not unless you want them to. The standard AR views open in the phone browser from a link or QR code, no install. If you choose the branded AR app service, your shoppers use your own app instead.

How long before my AR is live?

For the standard catalogue route, about seven days from your photos arriving. For custom services it depends on what we are building. Ask for a written timeline for your store before you commit, and we will give you one.
