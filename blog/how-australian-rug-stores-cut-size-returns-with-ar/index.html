How Australian Rug Stores Cut Size Mismatch Returns With True-Size AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a rug store owner in Melbourne. She sells hand-knotted rugs online and from a showroom in Brunswick. Her customers love the patterns. What they hate is the guessing. A rug that looks generous in a photo can land too small in their living room. A rug that looks subtle online can arrive and swallow the whole space.

So she watches the returns come back. Not because the quality was wrong. Because the size was.

This is the honest version.

Why size mismatch is the rug store's most expensive return

Rugs fail on size more than almost anything else a decor store sells. Furniture has standard sizes people know. A rug is pure proportion. Too small and it floats in the middle of the room. Too big and it creeps under the sofa and the sideboard and makes the room feel cramped. There is no almost right with a rug. It either sits right or it comes back.

And a rug return hurts more than most. Rugs are bulky. The freight both ways can eat half the margin before you count the re-photo and the relist. Worse, a shopper who got the size wrong once rarely reorders. The sale is not delayed. It is gone.

What true-size AR (Augmented Reality) changes

True-size AR (Augmented Reality) answers the one question the photo cannot: how big is this rug on my own floor. The shopper opens the link on the product page from her own phone, points it at the living room, and the rug appears at its true size between her sofa and her coffee table.

She checks the rug against the furniture she already owns. She sees whether the front legs of the sofa will sit on it. She walks around it. Two minutes, no tape measure, no guessing. A rug bought with that check is a rug that stays.

Where the links go

  • On every product page, next to the price. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-on-your-floor link beside the buy button turns a proportion guess into a two-minute check.
  • In your chat threads. A lot of Australian rug buying happens through Instagram DMs and chat. An AR (Augmented Reality) link shared in the thread answers the size question without a showroom trip.
  • On the rug tag in the showroom. A QR code on the tag lets a browsing customer check the piece at home and come back decided. Shoppers browse in person and decide at home. Meet them there.
