How Canadian Decor Stores Win Christmas Sales with AR (Augmented Reality)

Picture a decor store owner in Toronto. It is late November, and the biggest shopping weeks of the year are about to start. Shoppers in Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal and Calgary are hunting for gifts and for pieces to freshen the home before the family arrives. A woman in Vancouver wants a large mirror for the hallway before her parents visit. A couple in Montreal found the shop through a search and want a floor lamp for the living room. A man in Calgary has been watching one framed print for weeks and plans to buy it while the Christmas sales are on. They like the piece, they like the price, and they still hesitate, because a piece bought from a screen can arrive the wrong size for the wall, and at Christmas there is no time to send it back and try again. Here is how a true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view on the product page wins those Christmas sales before they are lost.

Why Christmas decor sales get lost

Three reasons drive them. First, the size. A mirror that looked right in a product photo can sit too small above the sofa, or run too wide for the wall, and the buyer only finds out when it is already hung. Second, the colour and the light. The piece in your bright showroom can look different in her living room light, and a warm gold on screen can read cold grey on her wall. Third, the clock. Christmas gifts have a hard date, so a shopper who is not sure waits, and a shopper who waits misses the sale and buys nothing. At Christmas all three hit at once, because shoppers are buying fast, for a fixed date, with no time to measure twice.

What changes when they see it at true size

A true-to-size AR (Augmented Reality) view answers all three doubts in about a minute, on her own phone, in her own room. She taps the link on the product page, points her phone at her wall, and the piece appears at its true size, right where it would hang. She steps back. She checks it against her sofa and her side table. She sees the colour in her own light, next to her own wall. Our designing department builds each view from the real listed dimensions of the piece, so the size on screen is the size that arrives at the door. One number worth knowing, and it is Shopify's own data, not ours: products with 3D or AR views convert about 94% better than products without. A Christmas shopper who can check the true size hesitates less, and hesitation is the whole problem in the busiest weeks of the year.

Where the view goes at Christmas

  • On every product page, next to the price. That is where the size doubt lives. A see-it-at-true-size link beside the buy button answers the question at the moment it matters.
  • In the Christmas email and the sale banners. The link opens on a phone, which is where your gift buyers browse.
  • On the price tags in the shop. A QR code on the tag opens the same piece in AR. A visitor checks it at home and orders before the sale ends.
